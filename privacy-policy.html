Privacy Policy for AI Empire Uploader

Last updated: August 2026

AI Empire Uploader ("the App") is a personal-use application that allows the account owner to publish video content to TikTok using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Information We Access

The App accesses only the minimum information necessary to publish content on behalf of the authenticated account, including basic account information (username, profile ID) via TikTok Login Kit, and permission to upload and publish video content via the Content Posting API.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through TikTok's API is used solely to authenticate the account and publish video content initiated by the account owner. We do not use this information for any other purpose.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties. No data collected through this integration is used for advertising or analytics purposes.

Data Storage

Authentication tokens are stored locally and securely on the account owner's own device. No user data is stored on external servers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Authentication tokens can be revoked at any time by the account owner through TikTok's account settings. Uninstalling or disconnecting the App removes all locally stored access tokens.
